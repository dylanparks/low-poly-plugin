# Hands

## The mitten

The era's hand: a palm, the four fingers as one group with two joints (the knuckles and one joint standing for the
middle and end joints), and a thumb of its own. Rings: the wrist, the palm, the knuckles, the fingers' middle joint, the
tips, a tip point. The first build: 9.5 cm palm, 4.5 cm to the middle joint, 5 cm beyond it; the palm 8.8 cm across.

## The thumb

**Anatomy** (joint centre to joint centre, as a share of hand length; Buchholz 1992 coefficients):

| Segment | Share | At a 19 cm hand |
|---|---|---|
| Metacarpal | 0.251 | 48 mm |
| Proximal phalanx | 0.196 | 37 mm |
| Distal phalanx, to the tip | 0.158 | 30 mm |

- **Reach.** The bones themselves are shorter (48, 31 and 21 mm): the joint-centre lengths include the joints and the
  pulp. Laid against the index, the thumb's tip reaches about 69% along the index's first segment.
- **The thenar eminence** (the ball of the thumb) runs from the wrist crease to the thenar crease (mean 7.4 cm). The web
  to the index spans about 6.8 cm in palmar abduction.
- **The thumb's ray is turned about 80-90 degrees** from the fingers': its nail faces out to the side, its pad toward the
  index (Brunelli 1999).
- **Its joints' axes are skewed and don't intersect** (Hollister 1992, 1995). For a low-poly rig: it flexes across the
  palm about its own axis, square to its length and its pad.

**Build.**
- **Base.** The quad on the palm's radial-palmar side between the wrist and palm rings opens.
- **Tube.** A four-sided tube runs from it through rings at the knuckle, the end joint and the pad to a tip; the pad's
  ring can be three points.
- **What the tube makes.** Its first stretch is the ball of the thumb, and its ulnar side the first web.
- **Faces.** Turn every new face outward from the thumb's axis.
- **Bones.** Three: base (carpometacarpal), knuckle, end joint. The skin ring at each joint is split 50/50.
- **Bind pose.** The metacarpal leans 22 degrees out from the index's line and 16 toward the palm; both free joints bent
  12 degrees.
  - **Too little palmward lean:** at 10 degrees its knuckle sank into the palm's side.
  - **Pad facing the wrong way:** with the pad toward the back of the hand, every curl bent the thumb into the palm.
- **Muscle.** Its girth follows the palm's with the body type, the thenar a little further off the palm on a bodybuilder.

| Cylinder grip, 3-4 cm handle | knuckles 50-60, middle 70-85, end 40-50 (interpolated between 10 and 60 mm handles) | base flexed about 38 and abducted about 21, knuckle and end joint about 11 | Shimawaki 2019; Curran 2019 |
| Key pinch | | base 31, abduction 8, knuckle 26, end joint 2; the pad on the index's side | Curran 2019 |

- **The fist.**
  - **Fold the middle joint deeply.** The mitten's middle joint folds past either real joint because it stands for two;
    at 106 degrees the fingertips stopped 2 cm short of the palm.
  - **Contact.** The fingertips press into the ball of the thumb, and the thumb lies over the fingers' middle segments.
    That is contact (`joints-and-motion.md`). Steered clear of the fingers, the thumb stood off the fist.
  - **Thumb outside.** A thumb tucked inside the fingers breaks on a punch.
- **The idle's curl comes from the measured relaxed posture.** At a quarter of it the hands hung nearly flat.
