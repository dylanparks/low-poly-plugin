# Common failures

The detail behind SKILL.md's "Common failures". Each was found in a real build (the two case studies have the numbers). The fix is in the section named.

| Failure | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| A cat's head that reads as a reptile | A long tapering snout with no stop, a flat forehead running into it, big eyes stretched along the sides, thin spike ears, no cheeks, pads or chin | Build the cat face's must-haves (`references/heads.md`, "A cat's head"); check it beside the reference photos front, side and 3/4 |
| A neck that is one ring from chest to skull | A sharp point at the forechest and a crease where the neck meets the head; from above, a neck as wide as the head | Two or more neck rings set from the reference's back line to its throat line, leaning back as they climb; flat- or round-fronted chest rings so no vertex juts forward; from above the neck pinches in behind the skull, narrower than the head and the shoulders |
| A breed's colour built on another breed's body | A flame point built on the modern show Siamese's tubular body and wedge head, where the user's cat was a sturdier traditional colourpoint | Translate the breed into its type before referencing (`lowpoly-core/references/measuring.md`, the translation table); the contract's colour profile never sets the body |
| A big wing built as hinged feather pages | Flat panels at rest however many rows are added; a shared fold corner drags one layer through the next (106-144 crossings), split corners leave slivers and dark hairlines at the elbow and wrist | One continuous surface with a trailing-edge bone per segment, and a separate sculpted shell for a rest pose it can't fold into (`references/parts.md`, "Wing on a quadruped") |
| A raised wing's underside reads as a black slab | The underside faces the ground, which the engine lights with the ground colour alone (0.21-0.36 of the top's brightness), though its normals are right | Shade a thin sheet's lower side with its upper side's normal; its own texture patch if it should read greyer |
| Flying legs hanging straight | In a hover the forelegs dangle forward instead of tucking, with nothing constraining them: the local joint turns cancel | Pose the tuck and measure the joint angles in the posed frame (`references/locomotion.md`, "Flying on a winged quadruped") |
| A head painted as smooth gradients, or a beak painted like feathers | A rebuilt eagle head in airbrushed white read as plastic next to its streaked neck (head fine detail 0.012); repainted to a feather target, its beak took the same blocky grain and read as feathered | Paint feathers, fur and scaled skin to about 0.03 fine detail (each texel's step from its 3x3 mean, over the mean), with strokes that follow the part's flow and match its neighbour's brightness; paint horn, beak, claw and hoof as smooth lit forms (a lit ridge, a sheen, a faint lengthwise streak, about 0.015), and gate the stroke layer off them by region, not by distance |
| A reptile belly drawn as crease lines | Thin dark lines and scribbled folds on a pale belly read as a sketch | Soft transverse rolls of about 15 texels: a groove on each ring (and one between), a soft U half a roll wide in the skin's own darker hue, a blurred highlight 40% down each roll, the belly shaded as a cylinder (`references/reptile-skin.md`, section 5) |
| Scales that read as cobblestones, and a belly with a hard edge | Round cells lit from their centres, black grout, no grain; the belly switched by which way each face points | Plates in a wavy diamond grid laid along the body, lit on the upper edge and shaded toward the trailing edge; the belly blended by position round the body over a wide band (`references/reptile-skin.md`) |
| A bird's beak drawn like a mammal's muzzle | A beak half the head or longer, its top edge sloping down from base to tip, as tall as the skull: a toucan, not an eagle | Measure the beak's landmarks in a side photo; the culmen level until the hook, the cere in front of the eye (`references/heads.md`, "Birds") |
| Buried limb tubes, capped tails | Attached when still, coming apart in motion; a visible gap at the tail | Holes and bridges (§3, rules 1, 2, 7) |
| Stick legs of even thickness | A rump overhanging thin legs; a V-notch between belly and thigh | Thigh ring from kneecap to hamstring, the flank fold (§3, rule 2) |
| A leg that leaves the belly as a thin column, no thigh | Built from a skeleton where the femur is inside the torso outline; the muscle reference was not checked | A thigh lobe ring under the hole, a mid-thigh ring tapering to the knee, a fold against the belly (§3, rule 2) |
| The thigh widest at the knee, the haunch pinched above it | From the front or behind, the leg's mass at the bottom: a bulging knee under a narrow haunch (`lp check`: `limbs.hind_peak` under 0.25, `limbs.hind_knee` over 0.95) | A ring at mid-thigh at the fullest point, the haunch's lower flank out, the knee in (`references/limbs.md`) |
| Feet built in the leg's frame | One corner under the ground, tilted soles | Flat soles at y = 0 on their own bone (§3, rule 5) |
| Paws built as upright boxes; a large clawed foot as a slab | Stumps: the same width at ankle and sole, no toes reaching forward, no claws | A sloping, splayed wedge; toe wedges and claw spikes where the species shows them (§3, rule 5) |
| A species' extreme feature left till last | A tube snout, plume tail or knuckle-walking forefoot that doesn't read | Measure and build it first, from more than one photo (lowpoly-core §1) |
| One ring profile for the whole head | A lower jaw of constant depth, a level mouth line, a jaw that runs into the neck: a small, slender relative instead of the species | Each jaw ring shaped from the jaw's bones and muscles (`references/heads.md`, "Jaws and mouths") |
| Equal teeth in both rows, evenly spaced | A zipper | The species' dental formula, size gradient and overlap (`references/heads.md`, "Jaws and mouths") |
| The head widest at the eye; a box-shaped lower jaw | A ball of a head on the neck; a jaw like a crate from the front | The widest point over the jaw muscles, a rounded jaw section (`references/heads.md`, "Jaws and mouths") |
| Rings tilted with a steep neck | The throat folds back under the jaw: a sheer drop from the jaw's corner | Rings kept in order along the body, upright where the loft climbs (`references/heads.md`, "Jaws and mouths") |
| Brow ring above the crown | An eyebrow lump over a sunken face | A convex head top line (§5) |
| Muzzle as wide and tall as the face | Eyes repeated on the snout | A muzzle block below and between the eyes, on its own patch (§5) |
| Ears as loose cards on a round skull | Blades seen from the side | Ears closed into a squared skull's corners (§5) |
| A scaled, wet or chitinous animal painted with fur's recipe | A lizard in fuzz, a beetle like felt: no cells, no crevices, no painted gloss | Name the skin per region and paint it to type (`references/skin-types.md`) |
| Stroke numbers judged with bold markings in | The checks report one-direction strokes, or a form layer too strong, on a coat whose strokes already follow a flow | Measure the strokes with the markings painted out, then put them back (`references/texture.md`, "Surface") |
| Fur noise frequencies swapped | Stripes across the body | Low frequency along the flow (§6) |
| Fur keyed to the true height on the unrolled back | Stripes across the top of the back and the forehead, seen from above | Key the strokes to the band's own height (`lowpoly-core/references/texture-method.md`, "Continuity") |
| Only the large light painted; strokes in one direction everywhere | A flat coat of parallel scratches; no shoulder, haunch or joint to be seen | The form layer and a stroke flow field, measured (§6) |
| A nose painted as one flat dark cap, the muzzle in the coat's flat colour | A hole or a smudge for a nose; a muzzle with no form, no mouth | The muzzle as a lit box: a lit nose top and nostrils, a lit bridge, a dark mouth line, a shaded chin (§6) |
| A lid drawn as a black ring of one strength all round | A thick, harsh outline round the eye, like a cartoon | A deeper shade of the coat, densest at the eye's edge and fading out, the upper lid stronger (§6) |
| An eye painted as one soft blob near the coat's value | Visible in the texture, gone on the model: no catchlight, no rim, a smear at game distance | The eye stack, crisp at the centre, measured round the eye (§6) |
| Eyes moved to a dead-forward surface to show from straight on | A painted visor | Eyes in the orbits, in an oblique patch (§5) |
| Parts overlapping in the side pelt | Ears or a far leg in the colour of what they cover | Their own strips (§6) |
| Forward- or back-facing faces on the side projection | Bands of smeared texture across a blunt nose, a chin or a chest front | Their own patch, projected along their direction, painted from the pelt unrolled round the edge (`references/texture.md`) |
| Toes as separate wedges sunk into the foot | Toes that slide through the foot in motion; no join between toe and foot | Toes welded onto the foot's own faces, each on its own bone (`references/feet.md`, "Claws and toes as part of the skin") |
| A reference model's construction copied without the species' anatomy | Clean claws on a foot that reads as spikes, or as a hand from below | The anatomy first (weight-bearing toes, the first toe, the heel pad), then the construction (`references/feet.md`) |
| Sine waves on each joint | Feet that slide and sink | Planted feet with IK, including the body's bob (§7) |
| Gait numbers in range, shapes unchecked | Stick hind legs nobody noticed | A frame-by-frame sheet against reference (§7) |
| One gait setting for every foot posture | Long flat soles dragging in the swing | Settings per leg kind and posture (§7) |

The failures that apply to any model, and the habits that catch them, are in
`lowpoly-core/references/common-failures.md`.
